## Lessons sent

- **semantic-content-writer**: a "X vs Y" or bare-noun keyword needs a carrier: a determiner before it and a head noun after it ("the X vs Y question"). Test: swap the keyword for "this choice" and read the sentence.
- **content-qa**: quote each sentence that holds the keyword (title, H1, description, first 100 words) in the report. FAIL it if the sentence is ungrammatical, even when `onpage` gives 100.
- **seo-keyword-researcher**: for comparison and bare-noun keywords, put a `keywordCarrier` sentence pattern in the brief, not just "exact keyword in the first sentence".
