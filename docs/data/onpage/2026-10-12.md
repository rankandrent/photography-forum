## Lessons sent

- **semantic-content-writer**: a "Why" H2's bold answer restates the subject and never opens with "Because". Includes a bad/good pair and a hide-the-H2 test.
- **content-qa**: an `awk` check for "Why" H2s whose answer starts with "**Because**". Also an **escalated** rule (2nd repeat: 10-11, 10-12): list every brief `backlinkSuggestions` entry with a status before PASS, because "optional" with no status is a FAIL.
- **seo-keyword-researcher**: give backlink suggestions a priority of `required`, `skip` (with a reason) or `conditional` (with a blocker), never "optional".
