## Lessons sent

- **seo-keyword-researcher (escalated to the top of the file, gap seen on 2 posts):** brief `entities` and `attributes` must be literal, printable forms. One name per entity, with brackets only at the end. An attribute label must be the noun the post will print. Good and bad examples are taken from both briefs.
- **semantic-content-writer:** run `npm run onpage -- /blog/<slug>/` before handoff. Put the brief's exact noun once into the sentence that already carries the fact, and never as a name-drop.
- **content-qa:** split every coverage miss into "covered, other words" (head-noun grep) and "not covered". FAIL only on the second group, with one line per item.
