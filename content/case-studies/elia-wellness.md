## Overview

Elia Wellness is a holistic wellness initiative by the Sydney Adventist Church. It wanted a user-friendly learning management system (LMS) based on its popular book *Live More*. The platform goes beyond traditional e-learning by building wellness into every module: from course creation to social connection, people track, share and celebrate their wellness journey in a responsive, engaging environment.

**Scope of work:** idea to product, strategy, web app, mobile app and website.

![Elia Wellness dashboard with a 7-dimension wellness score wheel, the Live More course, a leaderboard, the user's bucket list and challenge progress](/work/elia-wellness/cover.webp "The Elia Wellness dashboard")

## The challenge

Older adults often have limited access to wellness resources made for their needs. Many digital options are too complex, or they lack the support this group looks for. A wellness product for people over 50 had to:

- **Be intuitive** for people with very different comfort levels with technology.
- **Deliver meaningful, measurable wellness content**, not just lessons to click through.
- **Go further than standard e-learning tools**, with gamification, social connection and interactive wellness tracking that help older adults take charge of their health.

## Our approach

We took a human-centered approach focused on accessible, easy-to-navigate UI and UX. The design centered on responsive, mobile-friendly modules that keep engagement high. The platform is built around five experiences.

### Share and celebrate wellness milestones

A social feed lets learners post progress, react and reply, so the journey stays social.

![Elia Wellness social post: Marc James has joined the Personal Growth course, with a photo, likes, comments and a reply field](/work/elia-wellness/social-feed.webp "Share and celebrate milestones")

### Track the 7 dimensions of wellness

The wellness score shows how a person is doing across seven dimensions: emotionally thriving, growth mindset, actively engaged, environmentally attuned, socially connected, physically energised and spiritually empowered.

![Elia Wellness score wheel listing the 7 dimensions of wellness](/work/elia-wellness/wellness-score.webp "The 7 dimensions of the wellness score")

### Deliver engaging courses

Course creators build lessons from blocks (text, images, video and challenges), so every module can mix learning with a wellness activity.

![Elia Wellness course editor with an Add Block panel and a lesson called The Practice of Gratitude](/work/elia-wellness/course-editor.webp "Block-based course editor")

### Showcase achievements with badges

Lessons unlock items in the learner's bucket list, such as seeing a sunrise or a stretching day, which turn progress into small rewards.

![Elia Wellness mobile Badges screen with a bucket list of lessons: seeing a sunrise, stretching day and rest day](/work/elia-wellness/badges.webp "Badges and bucket list")

### Monitor progress

Learners see today's progress and their course stats week by week, which keeps the experience rewarding.

![Elia Wellness Today's Progress card at 50% and weekly course stats bar chart](/work/elia-wellness/course-progress.webp "Progress and course stats")

## The solution

The Elia Wellness platform brings together what learning and wellness both need, so the user journey feels seamless and rewarding:

- **Course creation** for authors, with lessons, modules and tutorials.
- **Performance tracking and interactive challenges** that keep people learning.
- **Wellness dimensions and scoring** that give each person their own metrics.
- **Gamification** such as badges that motivates users.
- **An integrated social feed** for sharing, which keeps the journey social and collaborative.

The course builder gives authors one place to structure lessons and modules.

![Elia Wellness desktop course builder showing lessons in the Live More course and the Practice of Gratitude overview](/work/elia-wellness/course-builder.webp "Course builder for authors")

On mobile, learners move between lessons and challenges, their wellness score and a personal growth questionnaire.

![Three Elia Wellness mobile screens: a lesson with challenges, the wellness score dashboard and a personal growth questionnaire](/work/elia-wellness/mobile-screens.webp "Mobile app screens")

The pre-course questionnaire works on tablets too, with large touch targets and simple rating scales.

![An older adult holding a tablet with the Elia Wellness pre-questionnaire on personal growth](/work/elia-wellness/tablet-questionnaire.webp "Pre-course questionnaire on a tablet")

## Results

Elia Wellness is more than a platform: it is a supportive digital ecosystem for building a healthier, happier community.

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| User registrations | +40%, growing from local origins to a worldwide wellness community |
| Wellness scores among active users | 30+ boost, reflecting reduced stress and improved resilience |
