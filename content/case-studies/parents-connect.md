## Overview

Parents Connect is a non-profit organization that empowers parents worldwide. Its mission is to give parents the education, support and resources they need to help their children succeed. We partnered with Parents Connect to design a digital platform where parents connect, share knowledge and build a global parenting community.

**Scope of work:** product revamp, discovery, stakeholder interviews, UX design, and brand and visual design.

## The challenge

Parents Connect needed one platform for parents from very different backgrounds across the globe. To grow a global community and maximize its impact, the platform had to:

- **Bridge the digital divide** for parents with different levels of digital experience.
- **Engage users deeply**, so parents return to the community and take part.
- **Be accessible to all**, whatever a parent's country, language or device.

## Our approach

We started with research into what parents need from a community. From there we designed the Parents Connect forum, the educational and inspirational content, and a space where parents interact and share experiences. The platform is built around five experiences.

### Expert advice and inspiring stories

Parents tune in to video episodes with expert advice and real stories.

![Parents Connect video episode card "New Generation of Education" with a play button](/work/parent-connect/expert-episodes.webp "Video episodes with expert advice")

### A global community

Parents find and follow other parents anywhere in the world.

![Parents Connect member pop-up showing Maria Lopez from Tokyo with a Follow button and member avatars](/work/parent-connect/global-community.webp "Find and follow parents worldwide")

### Chat, share and support

Real-time chat lets parents support each other one to one and in groups.

![Parents Connect desktop chat screen with a conversation list, messages and a member profile panel](/work/parent-connect/chat-desktop.webp "Chat with parents worldwide")

### Insights from parenting experts

Experts publish articles that parents read, react to and discuss.

![Parents Connect mobile article "Turning kids into Entrepreneurs?" with tags and reactions](/work/parent-connect/expert-article.webp "Expert articles on parenting topics")

### Share your thoughts

Comments and reactions turn every article and webinar into a conversation.

![Parents Connect comment card with reactions and a comment input field](/work/parent-connect/share-thoughts.webp "Comments and reactions")

## The solution

We designed a feature-rich platform with:

- intuitive navigation
- personalized content recommendations
- interactive forums for connection and advice
- expert-curated content on parenting topics
- a global events calendar that keeps the community engaged

The community feed brings posts, online members, the members directory and chats into one view.

![Parents Connect desktop community feed with posts, online members, a members directory map and chats](/work/parent-connect/community-feed.webp "Community feed")

The members directory helps parents find others nearby, filtered by distance and interest.

![Parents Connect members directory map with distance filters and a list of 16 search results](/work/parent-connect/member-directory.webp "Members directory")

The same experience works on mobile: the map, articles and chat.

![Three Parents Connect mobile screens: member map, expert article and chat](/work/parent-connect/mobile-screens.webp "Mobile app screens")

## Results

The design turned Parents Connect's vision into a working platform. It is now a source of support for parents worldwide and drives positive change for parents and children:

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Forum participation among parents | +45% |
| Average time spent on the platform | +10% |
| Parents reporting positive change in their children | 20+ |
| Parents reporting more confidence in parenting | 40+ |
