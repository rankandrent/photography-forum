## Overview

Vocable brings content operations into a single platform. It fulfills entrepreneur Iman Oubou's vision of empowering creators by centralizing the whole content lifecycle, from ideation to optimization. Creators get one seamless journey, so they can focus on their craft and publish content that resonates with their audience and drives content marketing results.

**Scope of work:** idea to product, stakeholder interviews, MVP launch strategy, UI and UX design, web app, and product enhancements.

![Vocable workspace with content drafts, a content score of 72, brand tone-of-voice sliders and a Start content writing with AI card](/work/vocable/cover.webp "The Vocable workspace")

## The challenge

Content marketers are under constant pressure to produce high-quality content quickly while hitting strategic goals. The work broke down in three places:

- **Researching trends** took hours before a single line was written.
- **Measuring performance** across tools was overwhelming.
- **Little time was left** for strategic planning and audience-centered content.

Vocable needed to automate that work, so writers could focus on compelling narratives that drive results.

## Our approach

Designed for humans, powered by AI. Vocable's interface puts every stage of content creation at the writer's fingertips, while AI takes the grunt work out of everything from auditing to drafting. With a streamlined workflow and data-driven insights, writers create content that performs.

### One editor, multiple AI models

Writers ask AI to edit or generate inside the editor and switch between models from OpenAI, Google, Anthropic and Meta without leaving the page.

## The solution

Vocable makes content creation far more efficient:

- **AI speeds up planning and drafting.**
- **One platform replaces the juggling** of multiple tools.
- **Collaboration and real-time refinement** keep content improving before it ships.

The result is less time per piece of content and a smoother workflow, so teams can focus on strategy and reach their content marketing goals faster.

The dashboard shows documents completed and in progress, active campaigns and the content plan in one view.

The editor keeps the content score, readability and SEO checks next to the draft, with collaborators working in the same document.
