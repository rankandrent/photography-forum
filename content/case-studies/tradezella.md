## Overview

TradeZella is a trading journal and analytics platform that gives traders one place for their whole workflow:

- real-time market data
- advanced charting tools
- order execution
- trade journaling
- in-depth performance analytics

Our goal was to make the platform easier to use and more accessible for novice and experienced traders alike.

**Scope of work:** product revamp, discovery, stakeholder interviews, UI/UX design, and web and mobile app design.

## The challenge

TradeZella had powerful features, but several UX problems held back satisfaction and engagement:

- **A complex, cluttered interface** that buried the numbers traders care about.
- **A steep learning curve** for new users.
- **No onboarding guide** to help first-time traders get started.
- **An inconsistent experience** across devices.

We worked with the founding team to take the product experience to the next level. Our main focus was the **dashboard analytics**.

## Our approach

We revamped the web and mobile experience around how traders actually review their performance. The work focused on five areas.

### Progress tracking with net daily P&L

Traders see at a glance whether each day was a winner or a loser, and how their account is trending.

![TradeZella Net Daily P&L bar chart with green profit and red loss days](/work/tradezella/net-daily-pnl.webp "Net daily P&L")

![TradeZella daily journal chart showing net P&L, total trades, win rate and gross P&L for a day](/work/tradezella/daily-journal-pnl.webp "Daily journal performance view")

### Analytical insights for scaling up

Key ratios like profit factor and win rate are surfaced as clear, glanceable widgets.

![TradeZella profit factor gauge and win-rate ring widgets](/work/tradezella/profit-factor-win-rate.webp "Profit factor and win rate widgets")

![TradeZella analytics widgets: net P&L card, account leaderboard, Zella Score trend and candlestick chart](/work/tradezella/analytics-widgets.webp "Analytics widget set")

### Journaling for mentors and mentees

Mentor mode lets a coach follow each mentee's cumulative P&L side by side, which makes feedback specific.

![TradeZella mentor dashboard comparing mentees' daily net cumulative P&L](/work/tradezella/mentor-dashboard.webp "Mentor's dashboard")

### Up-front visibility of the market and positions

Open positions and recent trades sit one tap away, and they are color-coded by profit and loss.

![TradeZella open positions table with dates, symbols and net P&L](/work/tradezella/open-positions.webp "Open positions and recent trades")

### Customisation to each trader's preferences

Traders arrange the widgets and views they need, including the Zella Score, their account balance and a P&L calendar.

![TradeZella Zella Score screen with score radar, performance chart, insights, account balance and P&L calendar](/work/tradezella/zella-score-screens.webp "Zella Score and customisable dashboard")

## The solution

We ran discovery sessions with the stakeholders to understand their long-term vision. Then we rebuilt the user flows around the features and widgets traders use most, so they can navigate faster and focus on trading profitably. The visuals follow industry best practices for financial dashboards and were tested with traders before launch.

![TradeZella marketing website on a laptop: all-in-one trading journal and analytics tool](/work/tradezella/website-current-market.webp "Marketing website")
