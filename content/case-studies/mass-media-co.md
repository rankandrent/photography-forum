## Overview

Mass Media Co. (MMC) provides data-driven digital marketing services. The team uses real-time data to make informed decisions and drive results. Its secure audience database holds verified personally identifiable information (PII) from various sources, which lets MMC build highly targeted campaigns that reach the right people with the right message.

**Scope of work:** product revamp, user interviews, information architecture (IA), UI and UX design, and web and mobile app design.

![Mass Media Co. platform screens: campaign planning, a reach report, an audience chart, a product table and a 250M audience figure](/work/mass-media-co/cover.webp "The Mass Media Co. platform")

## The challenge

The MMC team used several systems separately, which made the workflow slow and labor-intensive. The fragmented setup meant:

- **No consistent user experience** across the tools the team relied on every day.
- **Order takers struggled** to move between disconnected systems to fulfill customer orders.
- **A process that would not scale:** over time it became clear that one smart, efficient system could streamline order taking.

MMC looked for a partner to revamp both the process and the systems, and we worked with the MMC team on that full process transformation.

## Our approach

We ran a discovery workshop with MMC stakeholders and users to understand the business goals and align the product revamp strategy with them. The workshop covered product definition, strategy, information architecture and user flows. The result was an omnichannel platform that handles every marketing touchpoint, built around five experiences.

### Social media sales

Sales teams package and sell social campaigns, such as Facebook marketing, as clear products with goals attached.

![Mass Media Co. product card for Facebook Marketing Sales with Footer and Free package tags and an Explore More link](/work/mass-media-co/social-sales.webp "Social media sales packages")

### Campaign sales creation

Each campaign shows revenue, cost, total and order count at a glance, so the team sees how a sale is performing.

![Mass Media Co. Total Sales card showing $125 revenue, $98 cost, $2,000 total and 5,430 total orders](/work/mass-media-co/campaign-sales.webp "Campaign sales at a glance")

### Audience engagement reports

Reports break campaign reach down by device, age group and channel, so clients see who the campaign reached.

![Mass Media Co. annual report data breakdown with reach by mobile, desktop, tablet and social, and an audience summary chart by age group](/work/mass-media-co/audience-report.webp "Audience engagement report")

### Task management

Every creative task shows its owner, due date and progress, from in progress to completed.

![Mass Media Co. task cards: D&M Email Creative in progress at 70% and Dunder Memorial Day completed at 99%](/work/mass-media-co/task-cards.webp "Task progress cards")

### Product and order management

An order summary compares channels such as email, audio, video and H&V, so order takers see where volume comes from.

![Mass Media Co. order summary bar chart across email, append, look-alike, audio, video and H&V channels](/work/mass-media-co/order-summary.webp "Order summary by channel")

## The solution

The platform streamlines mass media marketing with:

- **Advanced data analytics** for precise audience segmentation.
- **Several media channels in one place**, for a cohesive campaign.
- **Personalized, compelling content** for each audience.
- **Real-time performance tracking** with dedicated client support.

Together these make marketing efficient and effective with less effort, and they bridge the gap between MMC's clients and their target audiences.

The task management dashboard brings sales operations, open tasks, task flow by client and recent tasks into one view.

![Mass Media Co. task management dashboard with task counters, an open-tasks-by-client chart, a task flow donut and a task table](/work/mass-media-co/task-dashboard.webp "Task management dashboard")

Order management lets the team build an order from data products, digital channels and data processing services, with the estimated cost shown as they go.

![Mass Media Co. order management screen for a Memorial Day sales order with product rows and an Add a new product panel](/work/mass-media-co/order-management.webp "Order management")
