## Who we are

UIUXDesignServices.us is a research-led UI UX design agency. Since 2017 our team has designed more than 200 digital products, from first MVPs to enterprise platforms used by thousands of people every day.

We are headquartered at 11921 Freedom Drive, Two Fountain Square, Reston, Virginia, and we work remote-first with product teams across the United States.

## What we do

We cover the full product design lifecycle in one team:

- **Research and strategy:** [UX research services](/services/ux-research-services/), [UX audit services](/services/ux-audit-services/), [usability testing services](/services/usability-testing-services/) and [UX consulting services](/services/ux-consulting-services/).
- **Product design:** [digital product design services](/services/digital-product-design-services/), interaction design and [design system services](/services/design-system-services/).
- **Platforms:** websites, web apps, and iOS and Android apps.
- **Dashboards and data:** dashboard UX, dashboard UI and data visualization.

See every engagement on our [UI UX design services](/services/) page.

## How we work

- **Research before pixels.** Every engagement starts with users, data and business goals, not assumptions.
- **Senior people on every project.** The designers who run your research stay through design, testing and handoff.
- **Measurable outcomes.** Each design decision ties back to a metric agreed at kick-off, such as activation, conversion or task success.
- **Built to ship.** Developers receive annotated Figma files, components and specs they can build directly.
- **Transparent pricing.** Engagements start at $5,000 for a UX audit, and every project begins with a free consultation and a fixed-scope proposal.
